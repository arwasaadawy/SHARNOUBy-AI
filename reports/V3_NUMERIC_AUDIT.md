# Exhaustive annual numeric reconciliation

All 80 available branch/category/year combinations passed 880 independent SQL/conservation checks.

Money tolerance: less than half a piastre (0.005 EGP); maximum observed floating-point difference: 0.000000029802 EGP. Distinct invoice counts and unit counts match exactly.

Verified invoiced net sales, event-date refunds, net after refunds, estimated sold COGS, estimated pre-refund gross profit, estimated result before manually entered adjustments, invoice aggregate, monthly aggregate and refund register. Includes full and partial source years; only complete years are labeled complete.

This verifies arithmetic against the supplied records, not authenticity of modeled source data, completeness of actual expenses, audited real profit or accuracy of future predictions.
